Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version: Subbit stores the business information you type into it so the app can work. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Who we are

Subbit is a product of Sola Scriptura LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Subbit website and app at subbitcrm.com. Questions go to support@subbitcrm.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name, and a password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider; we cannot read it.
  • Company details: your company name, logo, business phone, email and address.
  • Your team: the names, email addresses and roles of people you invite to your company.
  • Your business records: the contractors you work for and their contact details, work orders, job addresses, scopes and line items, schedules, completion photos and notes, your saved rates, and your invoices.
  • Contractors who use a job request link: the company name, contact name, phone, email and billing address you enter in your profile, and the jobs you send. No login is created. Your profile and your links are also saved in your browser on your own device.
  • Technical information: when you load the site, the companies that host it receive standard connection details such as your IP address and browser type.

We do not collect payment card numbers. When paid plans are available, card payments will be handled by a payment processor and the card details will go to them, not to us.

How we use it

  • To run the app: showing your jobs, building your invoices, and letting your team see what their role allows.
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else can see it

  • People in your company. Owners, managers and project managers see your company's records. Crew members see scheduled and recently completed jobs, without pay or rates.
  • Contractors you link. If you choose to link a contractor, that contractor's system can send you work orders and can see your responses, completion photos and notes, and the invoices you send them for those work orders. You can unlink at any time.
  • Companies that run the service for us. Supabase stores the database, logins and uploaded files. Resend delivers account emails. GitHub hosts the website. Google serves the heading font. Each receives only what it needs to do that job.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of Subbit.

Photos and logos

Completion photos and company logos are stored at web addresses that are long and very hard to guess, but they are not protected by a password. Anyone who has the exact link to a photo can open it. Do not upload photos that show anything you need to keep private.

Cookies and storage on your device

Subbit does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The app keeps you signed in by saving a login token in your browser's storage on your own device, and remembers a few settings there, such as your last export format. Signing out removes the login token.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your company's records within 30 days, except anything we are required by law to keep.

Your choices

  • You can change your company details and your name in the app at any time.
  • You can download your invoices from the Invoices screen.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing support@subbitcrm.com. Depending on where you live, the law may give you additional rights, and we will honor them.

Security

All connections to Subbit are encrypted. Each company's records are separated from every other company's at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed or lost, but we work to prevent it and will tell you if a breach affects your information.

Children

Subbit is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell account owners by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
