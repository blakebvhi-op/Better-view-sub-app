Built around how subs actually get paid.

You do the work, prove it is done, and bill the contractor. Subbit keeps those three things in one place.

One card per contractor

Open a contractor and see every job you have done for them, every invoice you have sent, and what they still owe you.

Work orders your crew can read

Each work order is laid out like an invoice, with items, details and quantities. It has no prices on it, so you can hand it to anyone on the job.

A price list for each contractor

Save the items you do and what you charge that contractor. Add them to a work order or an invoice in one tap.

Invoices that write themselves

When a job is complete, the invoice starts from the work order and fills in your saved rates. Print it, save it as a PDF, or download it for QuickBooks.

Photos before the job closes

A job cannot be marked complete without at least one photo of the finished work, so you always have proof when it is time to bill.

The right view for each person

Crew see the schedule and the work, and can mark jobs complete. They never see pay or rates. Managers see everything.

From first job to paid invoice.

Setting up takes a few minutes.

  1. Add a contractor

    Create your company, add your logo, then add the contractors you work for.

  2. Build the work order

    Pick items from your price list, set the date, and it is on the schedule for you and your crew.

  3. Complete and invoice

    Add photos, mark it complete, and send the invoice. Track it until it is paid.
